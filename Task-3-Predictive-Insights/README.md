# 📊 Predictive Insights for Collections Strategy



## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on predictive insights for customer delinquency and developing a responsible collections strategy for Geldium. It proposes using predictive modelling to identify customers who may need early payment reminders and support.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand potential factors associated with customer delinquency.
- Propose a measurable collections intervention.
- Develop a SMART recommendation.
- Consider fairness, transparency, privacy, and responsible AI practices.

## 🧠 Proposed Approach

**Logistic Regression** is proposed as an interpretable model for estimating delinquency risk.

Potential factors to validate against the actual dataset and model results include:

- Previous missed or late payments
- Credit utilization
- Affordability indicators, if available

*Note: These are candidate factors, not confirmed model findings. They must be validated against the actual Task 2 results.*

## 💡 Key Recommendation

Propose a **six-week SMS reminder pilot** for customers identified as high risk by a validated model.

The pilot would measure:

- 30-day delinquency rate
- Repayment outcomes
- Contact costs
- Customer complaints and opt-outs

Collections staff should review the proposed contact list before messages are sent.

## ⚖️ Ethical and Responsible AI

- **Fairness:** Check for potential bias and unequal outcomes.
- **Explainability:** Communicate risk estimates in plain language.
- **Human oversight:** Keep people involved in consequential decisions.
- **Privacy:** Use only authorized and necessary data.
- **Accountability:** Document decisions and monitor model performance.
